How to use Store Helep

1. Scan your records

Place your sales notebook or page on a flat surface. Open Scan from the bottom menu, tap Scan a page, then take a clear photo. You can turn on the flash if your phone supports it.

2. Review what we read

Our system reads the page and shows each line item. Check that dates, product names, quantities, and prices are correct. Edit any line that looks wrong before confirming.

3. Confirm to save

When everything looks right, tap Confirm & save. The records become official business entries in your dashboard. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

4. Manage products & stock

Go to Products to add the items you sell, set the cost price in FCFA (what you paid for one unit), and track stock levels. The selling price is not typed in — it is read from your scans. Low or out-of-stock items are highlighted so you can restock quickly.

5. View records & analytics

Records shows all saved transactions with filters for today, this week, or custom dates. Analytics shows sales trends, best-selling products, and estimated profit.
